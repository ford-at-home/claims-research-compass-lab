## Rental competitor benchmark

Only Benchmark competitor rates requests outside research. The supplier rate is never sent outside, researched or overridden. Public quotes must match area, exact dates, duration, class, seating, currency and complete fee context. Quote identity, URL, exact excerpt, timestamp, rate basis and availability/confirmation basis are retained. The comparator rejects missing/mismatched source terms; retrieval time is not quote time. Quotes older than the configurable-in-code 24-hour review window are marked stale/noncomparable (a demo review rule, not supplier policy). Public retail and integrated contracted bases remain distinct. Base totals and deltas exclude switching cost; switching cost is a separate explicitly simulated/local scenario. Missing competitor quotes stay unknown.

The supplier response is simulated in Fixture, unconfigured in Live. Fixture-versus-public comparisons never claim real savings. No booking, messaging, reservation changes, coverage or settlement decisions occur.
